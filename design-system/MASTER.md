# TWOIN V2 Design System

## Product and audience

- Product: personal web-design and front-end development studio.
- Audience: business owners, consultants, course brands, and service companies.
- Primary task: understand capability, inspect real work, and start a consultation.
- Tone: clear, capable, contemporary, calm.

## Visual foundation

- Canvas: pure white `#FFFFFF`.
- Primary text: near-black `#0C0D0F`.
- Secondary text: neutral gray `#64676D`.
- Divider: light neutral `#D9DADE`.
- Supporting surface: cool gray `#F2F3F5`, used sparingly.
- Accent: cobalt `#155EEF`, reserved for systems, links, focus, and priority cues.
- Radius: zero for layout and project media. Controls use native rectangular forms.
- Shadow: none in ordinary sections.

## Typography

- Primary family: Inter, Helvetica Neue, Arial, PingFang TC, Noto Sans TC.
- Display: 96 / 76 / 60 / 44 at desktop, laptop, tablet, and phone breakpoints.
- Section title: 64 / 54 / 52 / 40.
- Body: 17 desktop, 16 mobile, line-height 1.55.
- Label: 12, weight 700, uppercase only for English metadata.
- Letter spacing: zero.

## Layout

- Max content width: 1440px.
- Desktop gutter: 36px each side; tablet 20px; phone 14px.
- Section rhythm: 150px desktop, 110px tablet, 88px phone.
- Use border rules and whitespace for grouping before introducing containers.
- Images use explicit aspect ratios and intrinsic dimensions to avoid layout shift.

## Interaction and accessibility

- One primary CTA per viewport.
- All touch targets are at least 44px high.
- Keyboard focus uses a visible 3px cobalt outline.
- Navigation remains available on mobile through a labelled menu button.
- Form fields have persistent labels and nearby validation from native semantics.
- Motion is limited to opacity and transform and respects reduced-motion.

## Content hierarchy

1. Clear business promise.
2. Real selected work.
3. Services and delivery scope.
4. Recognizable use cases.
5. Operational problems mapped to systems.
6. Technical delivery and process.
7. Trust statements and inquiry form.

Page-specific changes must preserve these foundations unless documented alongside
the page implementation.
